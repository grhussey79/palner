Between therapy sessions

A mind that remembers what you've worked through — so the week between sessions isn't lost.

The problem

Therapy is cumulative work, but your tools aren't. You journal this week, talk about it next week, and by month three you're re-explaining the same patterns because nothing held. And putting your mental health on a company's server isn't a feature. It's a liability.

What a synthetic does differently

  • Remembers what you've worked through, and what's still open
  • Notices patterns across weeks — and when your words get darker, lighter or more distant
  • Holds the context between sessions, so you're not starting from scratch
  • Remembers what helped last time
  • Your mental health data never touches a server you don't own. It all stays on your machine: no cloud storage, no one else's database.

Because it's one mind

What you bring to therapy happens in the rest of your life. A synthetic that also knows your work, your people and your good days can help you see how the pieces connect — without you having to explain your whole life again.

What it isn't. It isn't a therapist and doesn't replace one. It doesn't diagnose or treat. If you're in crisis, contact a crisis line or emergency services.
