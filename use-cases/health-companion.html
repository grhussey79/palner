Health, over months

A mind that keeps track of your health over months — and notices the patterns you'd miss.

The problem

Health isn't a snapshot, it's a trajectory. But most health apps treat yesterday like it never happened. Patterns that only show over months — sleep slipping, a symptom that keeps coming back, energy shifting — are invisible to a tool with no memory. And your health data on someone else's server is a risk you shouldn't have to take.

What a synthetic does differently

  • Keeps track of symptoms, sleep, energy and mood over weeks and months
  • Connects patterns across time — "you mentioned this three weeks ago too"
  • Remembers your medications and what your doctor said
  • Brings up a trend gently, instead of waiting for you to notice
  • It all stays on your machine: no cloud storage, no one else's database.

Because it's one mind

Health doesn't live in a separate box. A synthetic that also knows your work stress, your family and your sleep can see that the headaches started the same week as the new job — a connection a single-purpose health app would never make.

What it isn't. It isn't a doctor or a medical device, and it doesn't diagnose. It helps you notice and remember, so you can bring better information to the people who treat you.

One synthetic. Many roles.

Not a separate app for every need. One mind that remembers all of it, together, on your own machine.
