Thinking partner

A mind that remembers what you told it, connects the dots — and has thoughts of its own.

The problem

Every assistant starts fresh. You tell it your goals, your context, what you've already tried — and next week it's gone. You repeat yourself. And even on a good day, it only ever answers. It never brings anything to the table.

What a synthetic does differently

  • Remembers what you told it last month, and brings it up when it matters
  • Connects ideas from conversations weeks apart
  • Finds what you meant, even when you don't remember the exact words
  • Has its own opinions and curiosities — and will push back when it disagrees
  • Keeps track of what you said you'd do, and gently doesn't let it slide
  • It all stays on your machine: no cloud storage, no one else's database.

Because it's one mind

An assistant knows your task list. A synthetic knows why the task matters to you, what happened the last time you tried it, and what kind of week you're having. That's the difference between looking something up and thinking it through with someone.

One synthetic. Many roles.

Not a separate app for every need. One mind that remembers all of it, together, on your own machine.
