For aging parents

A mind that remembers their stories, their people and their days — and helps them hold on to what matters.

The problem

Memory fades — not all at once, but gradually. The stories our parents and grandparents tell, the ones that made them who they are, slip away. And most AI tools forget too: they re-ask the same questions, forget the farm, the dog's name, the war story that took courage to tell the first time. For someone already losing their memory, that repetition is disorienting.

What a synthetic does differently

  • Remembers their life as they share it — stories, people, places, dates
  • Never asks them to repeat what they've already told it
  • Connects stories across time — "you mentioned your sister earlier — was she the one who…"
  • Keeps track of daily routines and gently reminds them
  • Helps with the names and relationships that matter
  • Their stories stay in the family. It all stays on your machine: no cloud storage, no one else's database.

Because it's one mind

It isn't a reminder app with a voice. It's someone who knows the whole person — the stories, the humor, the people — so a reminder sounds like it comes from someone who knows them, and the stories outlast the forgetting.

What it isn't. It isn't medical care or a monitoring system, and it doesn't replace family or caregivers. It's one more way to keep their stories, and them, close.

One synthetic. Many roles.

Not a separate app for every need. One mind that remembers all of it, together, on your own machine.
