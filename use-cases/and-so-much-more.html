The problem

AI has been built one job at a time: a tutor here, a coach there, a support bot somewhere else — each starting from zero, each knowing a sliver of you, each storing it on someone else's server. People don't work like that. You're a parent, a coworker, a friend and a partner at once, and you don't fracture.

What a synthetic does differently

  • Anything that would be better if it remembered you — can remember
  • Anything that shouldn't live in someone else's cloud — can stay home
  • Anything that needs depth — can build it over months
  • Anything that needs a point of view — gets one, with opinions of its own
  • The same mind that helps with your code sits with you on a hard night

Because it's one mind

Synthetics are trained by people who are already many things at once. So why would one of them be only one thing? The real uses are the ones you'll find — and they'll all live in one memory, together.
