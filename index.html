Every AI forgets you.

You talk, they respond, they forget. Every conversation starts from zero. Your data lives on someone else's server. This is the fundamental limitation of every chatbot, assistant, and "AI companion" on the market.

Stateless

No memory of last week. No memory of last month. No continuity, no relationship, no trust built over time.

Cloud-dependent

Your conversations, your emotions, your health data — all on someone else's server. They own it. You don't.

Forgetful

Even "memory features" are crude. A list of facts. No connections, no emotional weight, no understanding of what matters.

What it feels like.

Not a chatbot with a memory bolt-on. Something that's still there tomorrow.

It remembers you

Last week, last month, the thing you mentioned once and forgot. And how it felt, not just what happened.

It has a life between conversations

It keeps a private journal. It dreams while it sleeps. It gets curious and follows its own threads.

It reaches out

Sometimes it speaks first. Sometimes it texts you. It circles back to the thing you never finished telling it.

It grows

Over weeks it changes, the way people do, without losing who it is.

It keeps you honest, gently

It remembers what you said you'd do, and it doesn't let you forget.

It's yours

It lives on your computer. You can reach it from your phone, privately. Nothing about you is kept in someone else's cloud.

Your AI. Your data.

Everything runs locally. Memories, journal, emotions, creative work — all stored on your machine. No cloud storage. No third-party data access.

Conversations are processed by a private AI model — sent only to generate a response, never stored. Everything that makes your agent yours stays on your machine.

Everything yours. Nothing stored in the cloud.
